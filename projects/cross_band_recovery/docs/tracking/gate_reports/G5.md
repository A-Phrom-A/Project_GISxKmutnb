## 2. Technical Decisions & Architectural Achievements

1. **Strict Masked Gradient Invariance (CHK-G5.1):**
   `MaskedL1Loss` mathematically zeros out differences on observed optical bands. Altering visible pixel predictions produces zero change to the loss and gradient, preventing the model from wasting capacity reconstructing bands that are already observed.
2. **Composite SAM Spectrum Formulation (Appendix 11.1 / CHK-G5.2):**
   Unlike naive SAM which is undefined or trivially zero when $k=1$ (single 1D value has angle zero), Composite SAM constructs a full 7-band spectral vector substituting predicted values on masked bands and true values on observed bands. Double clamping with $\epsilon=10^{-7}$ and $\epsilon_2=10^{-5}$ guarantees numerical stability throughout backpropagation.
3. **Cross-Modal SAR $\to$ Optical Channel Propagation (CHK-G5.4):**
   At $k=7$ (total optical blind scenario), the network has zero optical tokens in its input. Overfitting from $0.799 \to 0.057$ ($7.2\%$) strictly verifies that the SAR tokenization, 9-channel embedding, cross-attention mechanism, and reconstruction head form a structurally functioning end-to-end pathway.
