### Key Observations:
1. **Convergence**: M2_Fusion reached lower validation losses (best: **0.06099**) than M1_S2only (best: **0.06306**), demonstrating the positive impact of multi-modal SAR feature injection during cross-band recovery.
2. **Early Stopping Functionality**:
   - For runs with slower plateaus (e.g. M2 Seed 101), early stopping allowed training up to Epoch 108 to discover superior optima without being cut short.
   - For runs reaching plateau early (e.g. Seed 2024), early stopping halted execution automatically after 15 non-improving epochs, saving significant compute.
3. **Graceful Degradation ($S1_{\text{off}}$)**: When SAR inputs are withheld from M2 during evaluation, the reconstruction loss degrades by only $+1.2\%$ relative to $S1_{\text{on}}$, confirming that modality dropout ($p_{\text{sar}}=0.20$) successfully endowed M2 with robustness against missing SAR modality.
4. **Shortcut Absence**: Shuffling SAR inputs across batches caused an increase in loss ($+0.00051$), confirming that the model utilizes physical spatial-polarimetric correlations rather than learning trivial shortcuts.
