## 2. Downstream Segmentation Performance Across Conditions

All metrics below are computed over held-out spatial test tiles containing confirmed oil palm parcel annotations (4,726 GT parcels) evaluated through the frozen downstream `ViTPalmModel`:

| Condition | Description | Mean F1-Score | 95% Bootstrap CI | Mean IoU | 95% Bootstrap CI | Retention vs Clean |
|:---:|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **(A)** | **Clean (Pristine S2 Ground Truth)** | **0.5206** | $[0.4775, 0.5610]$ | **0.3813** | $[0.3443, 0.4161]$ | $100.0\%$ (Upper Bound) |
| **(B)** | **Unrepaired (Mean-Fill Occluded Bands)** | 0.2779 | $[0.2344, 0.3269]$ | 0.1910 | $[0.1581, 0.2267]$ | $53.4\%$ ($-46.6\%$ loss) |
| **(C)** | **Baseline B3 (Random Forest Imputation)** | 0.0267 | $[0.0205, 0.0335]$ | 0.0139 | $[0.0106, 0.0175]$ | $5.1\%$ (Severe distortion) |
| **(D)** | **M1 Repair (Optical-Only CrossBandViT)** | 0.4401 | $[0.3989, 0.4801]$ | 0.3070 | $[0.2743, 0.3389]$ | $84.5\%$ ($+16.22\%$ over Unrep) |
| **(E)** | **M2 Repair (Cross-Modal Fusion ViT + SAR)** | **0.4537** | $[0.4106, 0.4938]$ | **0.3198** | $[0.2861, 0.3525]$ | **87.1%** (**+17.58% over Unrep**) |

> **Key Scientific Insights:**
> 1. **Catastrophic Impact of Unrepaired Cloud Occlusion:** When optical vegetation bands (NIR/SWIR) are lost, the downstream palm segmentation model suffers a catastrophic drop from F1 $= 0.5206 \to 0.2779$ (a $46.6\%$ relative collapse in detection accuracy).
> 2. **Failure of Naive Imputation (B3):** Simple per-pixel regression fills pixel values without spatial structural context, resulting in unnatural spectral slopes that cause the convolutional downstream decoder to miss almost completely (F1 $= 0.0267$).
> 3. **ViT Cross-Band Recovery Restores Parcel Delineation:** M1 optical-only recovery restores F1 to $0.4401$ ($84.5\%$ of clean).
> 4. **Incremental Benefit of SAR Fusion:** M2 Cross-Modal Fusion achieves the highest downstream performance (F1 $= \mathbf{0.4537}$, IoU $= \mathbf{0.3198}$), delivering an additional **$+1.36\%$ absolute F1 gain** over optical-only M1 by incorporating radar canopy roughness and volume scattering.
